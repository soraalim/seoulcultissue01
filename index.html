Seoul Cult — Issue 01 2026

Editorial

Letter from the Editor

An intense sense of aliveness

Seoul became Ida Kymmer's new hometown in 2008. Back then, many people in Europe couldn't point out Seoul on a map. As the city's global fame grows, Seoul Cult invites readers deep into the culture and society, curating what is beautiful, delicious, cool, interesting and new, far from algorithms.

Retail

Brave Seoul Retail

Sonia Brown's exploration of Seoul's retail

Sonia Brown, Executive Director and Head of Design at Quadrant Design in London, travels to Seoul to experience its vibrant retail scene, visiting stores in Seongsu, Sinsa, Gangnam and beyond. “There is a real kind of bravery around Seoul, and I absolutely fell in love with it,” she says. Photography by Sonia Brown.

Tourism

Becoming Seoul

Travel to be someone new

K-pop, K-dramas and K-beauty have changed why people visit Seoul. Tourists arrived in record numbers, 18.9 million in 2025, the highest in South Korea's history, and most are here to try something on: a new skin routine, a wardrobe, a lifestyle, an identity.

Performance

Link Seoul

Contemporary and traditional

Link Seoul's EP:2, TALCHUM, takes Bongsan Talchum, a National Intangible Heritage mask dance of satire and provocation, and treats it as a living argument rather than an artefact, staged in two halves that interrogate each other.

K-Beauty

All Live Better

Olive Young's strategy for wellness and global expansion

CJ Olive Young opened Olive Better, a wellness store in Seoul's Gwanghwamun district with around 3,000 products from over 500 brands across more than 430 square meters, covering inner-beauty foods, supplements, fitness gear, aromatherapy, dermacosmetics and sleep enhancers.

Fashion

Structure and Culture

Report from Seoul Fashion Week F/W

At Seoul Fashion Week, designers showed tailored structures, play with soft materials, furs and frills, and strong references to Korean culture, with Yungadang among the names covered.

Architecture

A Radically More Human Seoul

Heatherwick Studio's work in Seoul

Heatherwick Studio headed the Seoul Biennale of Architecture and Urbanism and has since won projects including Nodeul Island, Hanwha Galleria, the COEX Convention and Exhibition Centre, and residential redevelopments in Daegyo and Apgujeong, aiming for a more human approach to urban development.

Curated

Keepsakes

Brave products for a curious city

Seoul's retail is brave in product development as well as interiors. A curated selection of ceramics, fragrance, skincare, leather goods, coffee and fashion from Korean brands such as Lee Eun Sang, Ryu, Haruharu Wonder, Tamburins, Kethy and Cueren.

Sports

The Olympic Medals and the Buddhist Monk

How Buddhism saved Korean snowboarding

South Korea won three snowboarding medals at Milano Cortina: Choi Gaon gold in women's halfpipe, Kim Sang-gyeom silver in men's parallel giant slalom and Yoo Seung-eun bronze in women's big air. The story traces back to a Buddhist monk and a snowboard competition sponsored by Korea's largest Buddhist order.

Hotel

The Bukcheon Village Hotel

A distributed hanok concept

Across restored hanoks in Jongno and Bukchon, IWA runs a “distributed village hotel” where the neighbourhood becomes the hotel: the lobby is a café down the alley, the room a century-old hanok, with floor heating, open-air baths and its own fragrance line.

AI

Protection Against the Internet's Worst Tendencies

Korea's AI Basic Act

On 22 January South Korea became the first country to put a comprehensive national AI law into full effect. The Basic Act on the Development of Artificial Intelligence runs to 43 articles across six chapters and mandates watermarking of AI-generated content, following a surge of deepfake crimes.

Fashion

Bouquet Collection Launch

Gentle Monster's new collection

Gentle Monster launched its 2026 Bouquet Collection in January with a campaign film co-directed by FKA twigs and Jordan Hemingway, set to an unreleased FKA twigs track, and immersive blossom-filled pop-ups in six cities.

Entertainment

What We Are Watching

Streaming on Netflix

The Art of Sarah, an eight-episode K-drama thriller; Park Chan-wook's No Other Choice; and The Philosopher's Kitchen, a documentary series on Jeong Kwan.

Books

Jeong

Korean design philosophy

Phaidon's Jeong, by Pentagram designer Hyo Jung Lee, is the first book to map the full breadth of Korean design: 3,000 years through 175 objects, organised around jeong, the Korean idea of connection, empathy and devotion between maker, material and user.
